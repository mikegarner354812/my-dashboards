Cross Harp Finder

Pick the key you want to play blues in. This shows which harmonica to pick up for 2nd position, and where the I, IV, and V chords sit on the circle of fifths.

I — the blues key (home) IV — your harmonica's key V — dominant

Why this works

  1. 2nd position (cross harp) means the harmonica's home key sits a fifth below the key you're playing in.
  2. So the harmonica you need is always the IV chord of the blues key — one step counterclockwise on the circle.
  3. The V chord is the mirror image — one step clockwise, a fifth above the blues key.
  4. Same three-in-a-row rule as any 12-bar blues: pick I, its neighbors are IV and V.
