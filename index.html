Mike's Music Project

Blues Theory Reference

A small set of interactive tools for harmonica, piano, and blues theory. Listed alphabetically.

Cross Harp Blues Scale →

Pick a blues key and see the extended blues scale mapped directly onto the harmonica holes — blow, draw, and bends — for 2nd position.

cross-harp-blues-scale.html

Cross Harp Finder →

Pick the key you want to play blues in and find which harmonica to grab for 2nd position, plus where I, IV, and V sit on the circle of fifths.

cross-harp-finder.html

Key Finder →

Enter the number of sharps or flats you see at the start of a piece and get the two candidate keys — plus what to check to pick between them.

key-finder.html

Key Signature Builder →

Pick a key and major or minor, see the scale with sharps/flats highlighted, and see the correct key signature drawn on a real staff.

key-signature-builder.html

Scale Builder →

Major → natural minor → minor pentatonic → blues, built up degree by degree in any key.

scale-builder.html
