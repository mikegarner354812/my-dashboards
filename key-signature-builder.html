On the Staff

The key signature goes once, right after the clef. Every note on that line/space is altered for the rest of the piece — that's why none of the individual noteheads below need their own sharp or flat mark.

Reading the signature

  1. Sharp order — F C G D A E B (always added in this order, left to right)
  2. Flat order — B E A D G C F (the exact reverse of the sharp order)
  3. Count the marks at the start of the staff to know how many degrees are altered
  4. Relative major/minor pairs (e.g. G major / E minor) always share the same signature
