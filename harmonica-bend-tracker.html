Harmonica Draw Bend Tracker

Track how your draw bends are going on every key of diatonic harmonica. Every cell starts blank. Click a cell to give it a color: red, then yellow, then green. One more click clears it back to blank. Untick any keys you don't own in the "My harmonicas" section below.

My harmonicas

Tick the keys you own and they appear in the chart above. Unticking a key hides its row but keeps its progress.

Where is my progress saved? Your progress is saved automatically in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser cache or site data, you will lose it. To keep it safe, or to move it to another device or browser:
  • To save: click "Download a backup copy". A file called bend-progress-backup.json will be saved to your computer. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • To load: click "Import a backup" and choose that file. It replaces what is currently on the page.
